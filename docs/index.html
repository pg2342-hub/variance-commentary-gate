Variance commentary gate

Drafts each department's monthly budget-vs-actual commentary from NetSuite-style ledger lines, with the query behind every figure. It stops on a bad extract, ties every figure to the trial balance, and holds the risky lines for FP&A before anything sends on its own.

Why it matters

A wrong cause sent to a department head costs FP&A more trust than the hours the draft saves. So the drafter only writes a number it can trace to a query, refuses to draft on an extract that does not match the ledger, and keeps three kinds of line on approval even after go-live: a variance above $40K, an account with no budget, and any entry posted after the books locked.

A line of commentary is written when a department and account is at least $5K and 5% off budget, or has spend with no budget. Everything else stays out of the email.

Back-test: does FP&A keep the cause?

Before a live close, the drafter reruns the last three closes and FP&A compares its causes with what they actually sent, for the 20 largest variances in each. The plan's bar is 18 of 20 in every close. Kept here: 20, 18, 19.

FP&A kept the drafted causeFP&A changed itHover a square for the variance.

Every change is something only FP&A could know from outside the ledger:

  • October, Customer Success, Travel. Drafted as vendor spend (Concur travel); FP&A knows it is a Sales travel invoice coded to Customer Success, which shows on both departments.
  • October, Sales, Travel. Drafted as vendor spend (Concur travel); FP&A knows it is a Sales travel invoice coded to Customer Success, which shows on both departments.
  • November, People, Software subscriptions. Drafted as vendor spend (Rippling); FP&A knows it is timing, a late invoice from the month before posted with this one.

Data

All data is synthetic: a made-up freight forwarder with 8 departments, a US and a European subsidiary, NetSuite-style journal lines, a trial balance and a vendor-level budget. Vendor names are public software and services used as realistic payees. FP&A's answer key in the back-test is invented, with planted items only FP&A would know.

Run it yourself

Python 3.9+, PyYAML and golive-gate.

pip install -e ".[dev]"
python -m vcg --sql
golive run eval/golive.yml
python -m vcg.backtest
